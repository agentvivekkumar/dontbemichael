## 5. Membership Cancellation, Freeze & Refund Policy
* **Cancellation Policy:**
  * Requires 14 days written notice via email or front-desk form prior to the next billing date.
  * No cancellation or early termination penalties.
* **Membership Freeze (Pause):**
  * Members may place their membership on temporary freeze for medical or travel reasons.
  * Minimum freeze duration: 14 days; Maximum: 60 days per calendar year.
  * Requires 7 days notice. A $10/month rate-hold maintenance fee applies during the freeze.
* **Refund Policy:**
  * Initiation fees and past monthly dues are strictly non-refundable.
