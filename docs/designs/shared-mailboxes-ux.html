Mailbox access: two ways to show it

Same rules as today: one team member watches each mailbox, and others can be allowed to send from it without reading it. What changes is where you set it and how it reads. Both mockups show Dwight, who watches sales@ and sends outreach from ceo@ (which Pam watches).

A) Each mailbox Dwight uses, with Inbox and Sending Recommended

No on/off switch. Email is a short list of addresses, and each one answers two questions: does Dwight watch its inbox, and how does his email from it leave. Send only reads as "Pam watches the inbox", so nothing implies Dwight is cut off from email.
