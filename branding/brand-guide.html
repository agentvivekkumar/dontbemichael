Brand guide, v2 Studio, 2026

Michael finally learned to delegate.

How the Don't Be Michael brand looks, sounds and moves: in the app and on dontbemichael.com. The full system, every token and component, is in DESIGN.md. This is the short version.

01 The idea

The office Michael should have run.

Michael is the boss who kept every job on his own desk. Don't Be Michael is a team of AI agents that does the work, with Michael as the office manager who finally delegates. The owner stays in control: only what Michael can't settle comes to them.

Every design choice says one of two things. The work is delegated. Or you stay in control.

One system, two scales

The app and the website use the same colors, type and illustration. The app is dense; the website is large. There is no louder web palette: coral always means "needs you", green means "done", blue means "working".

02 The logo

The Struck M, and the name in Sora.

A pixel M with a coral slash: "don't be M". It is the one pixel element in the brand, on purpose. The name is set in Sora SemiBold, sentence case, with the apostrophe.

Rules

  • Use the SVG, or a PNG at a multiple of 16 px. Minimum 16 px.
  • Keep 2 grid cells (1/8 of the mark) clear on every side.
  • The slash is always #FF6B6B. The M is #1A1320 on light, #ECEAF4 on dark.

06 The studio

An office you can watch.

The studio is the app's home screen and the website's hero: an isometric office with one pod per department and Michael's glass pod in the middle. Work arrives at the mailbox posts, goes to Michael, and travels to whoever's job it is. It is a status display, not decoration.

07 Voice

Short, plain, a little dry.

  • Name the actor: "Oscar is matching September payments", not "the agent is processing".
  • Owner words, not developer words. Fields first; explanations behind an info icon.
  • Michael speaks for himself on his cards: "I can't approve money."
  • Office humor only where nothing needs doing: idle quotes, empty states. The joke is on Michael, never the customer.
  • No em dashes, no en dashes, no hyphens used as dashes. No emoji in copy.
