Brand guide

The short version of the Don't Be Michael design system, for anyone making something with the brand: a slide, a sticker, a partner page, a new screen. The full rules are in DESIGN.md in this folder.

EDITION 2026-09-24
01 · THE IDEA

Michael kept every job on his own desk.

He delegated nothing that mattered and called it leadership. Don't Be Michael is the office he should have run: a team of AI agents that does the work, while the owner signs off on it.

Everything the brand makes should say one of two things: the work is delegated, or you stay in control by approving it.

02 · THE LOGO

The Struck M

A pixel M with a coral slash through it. Read it as "don't be M". It is built on a 16 × 16 pixel grid and is always drawn as square pixels, never smoothed.

COLORS ARE FIXED

M in ink #1A1320 (cream #FFFDF5 on dark). Slash always coral #FF6B6B. The logo never changes color with a theme.

SIZE

Smallest: 16 px. Use sizes that are multiples of 16 (32, 48, 64 and up) or the SVG, so every pixel stays square and sharp.

CLEAR SPACE

Keep 2 grid cells (1/8 of the mark's width) empty on every side. Nothing else goes inside that margin.

03 · LOGO DON'TS

Leave the mark alone.

rotate it
recolor it
blur, glow or soft shadow
put it on coral
  • Don't stretch it, outline it, or redraw the slash as a smooth line.
  • Don't use a character portrait, a necktie or a rubber stamp as the logo. Those ideas were tried and retired.
  • Don't use NBC or The Office logos, title lettering, cast photos or likenesses anywhere. Michael is an archetype, not a licensed character.

06 · VOICE

Short, human, a little dry.

ALWAYS

  • Name who is acting: "Oscar is reconciling March", never "the agent is processing".
  • Keep system messages under 12 words, in second person.
  • Aim the joke at Michael, never at the customer.
  • Stay honest: no price on a page without prices, no download button before a real release.

NEVER

  • Em dashes or en dashes. Use a period, comma, colon or parentheses.
  • Emoji in copy.
  • Exclamation marks, except for completions and notifications.
  • "Dont". Real apostrophes, always.
