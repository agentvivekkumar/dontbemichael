# Asset attribution

The only bundled assets are fonts (see `fonts/`, each with its own licence file).

The studio is drawn in code (`scene/studio/`), and team member avatars are
letters in their department's colors. Nothing here is third party art.

The pixel office floor, its LimeZu tilesets and its Tiled maps were removed on
2026-09-30 with design v2 (branding/DESIGN.md). If you are reading an older
commit, that is the change you are looking at.
