# Don't Be Michael v0.0.3

**An AI office for your small business.** Pick your kind of business, pick your team, and Michael, your office manager, runs the floor while you run the business.

## What's new in 0.0.3

- **Michael and your team get new instructions.** Michael runs the team as your office manager and brings you only what needs you. Each team member knows its job, who it reports to and how to hand work back. Your existing team gets the new Role description and Work style once, with the team list backed up first.
- **Every folder is private.** A team member opens only its own folder. Michael works in your business folder and can read his team's work, but not change it.
- **A company profile every agent works from.** Setup and Settings ask for your business essentials, and every agent gets them at the start of its work.
- **Memory that improves instead of piling up.** Each agent keeps a short list of lasting notes that a background tidy up sorts, merges and keeps current. Your Ask me answers go back to whoever asked, and are remembered.
- **A fresh start for idle team members, without losing work.** A large conversation that sits idle with nothing open is cleared after the agent writes a handoff, and you can bring it back.
- **Schedules name the job.** A schedule says when and which job; the agent does it the way its Work style says.

## Installing on your Mac

1. Open the downloaded `.dmg` and drag **Don't Be Michael** into your Applications folder.
2. Open it from Applications. The first time, macOS says it could not verify the app. Click **Done**.
3. Open **System Settings**, then **Privacy & Security**. Scroll down to the message about Don't Be Michael and click **Open Anyway**, then confirm.

You only do this once. macOS asks because this early build is not yet signed with an Apple Developer ID. A signed build is on the way.

You also need [Claude Code](https://docs.claude.com/en/docs/claude-code) installed and signed in.

When a new version comes out, the app tells you and links to the download. Install it the same way.
